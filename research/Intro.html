Introduction to ESports




ESports, short for electronic sports, are online gaming competitions in which professional or amateur players compete to win often monetary prizes. They have gained a lot of popularity during the start of the 21st century, and have become a major part of the sports and gaming industry. The research paper will explore the applications of computers and computer science in the industry, the career opportunities within, and the sustainability of ESports for the future.
