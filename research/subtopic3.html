Computer components

What are They? Computer hardware determines how fast your computer performs and executes functions. In ESports this is crucial so players need to pick the right components in their computers. The main components that are important in gaming are the central processing unit, the graphics processing unit, the memory, and the motherboard.

How it relates to CS: Computer science is a tool that allows us to study computer components and choose the rigt pc build. This is very helpful for gamers that want to optimize speed,performance,quality and their gameplay.
